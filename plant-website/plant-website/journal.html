Care guides

Short, practical advice for the plants we sell.

Monstera care

Bright, indirect light and a steady routine keep it growing.

See the care steps
  • Place it near a bright window, out of harsh midday sun.
  • Water when the top 3 to 4 cm of soil is dry.
  • Wipe the leaves monthly so they can absorb light.
  • Give it a moss pole as it grows taller.

Snake plant care

The classic low-effort plant, as long as you resist overwatering.

See the care steps
  • Fine in low light, happiest in bright indirect light.
  • Water every 2 to 3 weeks and let the soil dry fully.
  • Use a gritty, well-draining mix and a pot with holes.
  • Yellow, soft leaves usually mean too much water.

Money plant care

Grows in soil or a jar of water and is very forgiving.

See the care steps
  • Give it bright, indirect light.
  • In soil, water once the top layer feels dry.
  • In water, change it every 1 to 2 weeks.
  • Trim long vines to keep it full.

Jade and succulent care

Strong light, little water and fast drainage.

See the care steps
  • Give it as much bright light as you can.
  • Water deeply, then wait until the soil is completely dry.
  • Use a gritty mix and a drainage hole.
  • Go lighter on watering in the rainy season.

Flowering plant care

Sun and feeding decide how many flowers you get.

See the care steps
  • Bougainvillea and hibiscus need 5 to 6 hours of sun.
  • Let bougainvillea dry out between waterings for more blooms.
  • Feed every 2 to 3 weeks while in flower.
  • Prune lightly after a flush of flowers.
