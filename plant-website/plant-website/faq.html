Questions, answered

The things people ask us most.

How do I order?

Add plants to your cart and send the order to us on WhatsApp. We confirm availability, delivery charges and payment details with you there.

How long does delivery take?

This depends on your city. We share the expected time when we confirm your order.

What if my plant arrives damaged?

Send us a photo on WhatsApp within 7 days and we will replace it.

Are the plants pet-safe?

Each plant page lists whether it is pet-safe. Many popular houseplants are not, so check before you order.

Do you sell pots separately?

Yes. See the Pots category in the shop.

Can you help me choose a plant?

Yes. Send us a photo of your space on WhatsApp and we will suggest three plants.
