Grown with patience, shipped with care.

Verdance is a small nursery that grows plants for real homes, balconies and desks.

Why we started

We were tired of plants that looked great in the shop and gave up within a month. So we grow our own, acclimatise them to Indian light and heat, and only send what we would keep ourselves.

Every order comes with a care card and a real person on WhatsApp who can tell you why a leaf is turning yellow.
